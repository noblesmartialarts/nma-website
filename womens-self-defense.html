🤍Supportive and beginner-friendly. Anyone can sit out or modify any drill, any time, no explanation needed.

Questions

Do we need any martial arts experience?

No. The workshop is built for complete beginners — moms, teens, and women who have never thrown a punch. Everything is taught step by step.

Is it right for teens and their moms together?

Yes — that's the format this workshop is built around. Moms and daughters usually practice as partners, and the session ends with a private safety agreement you make together. It works just as well for women's groups and friends.

Is there sparring or hard contact?

No sparring. Techniques are practiced with control, on pads and with your partner, and anyone can sit out or modify any drill at any time — no explanation needed.

What should we wear and bring?

Comfortable workout clothes, sneakers (or bare feet on mats), a water bottle, and hair tied back. Please leave rings and other jewelry at home.

Where does the workshop happen?

We come to you — a church hall, school gym, studio, or community room in Wake Forest, Raleigh, Durham, and surrounding areas. Not sure where to host? Ask and we'll help figure it out.

Is there a waiver?

Yes. Before the workshop, your organizer shares one link and everyone completes a short online agreement on their phone. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18. Permission for photos or video is a separate, optional choice.
